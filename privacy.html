Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how KDO Digital ("we", "us", "I") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit this website or get in touch. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in line with the Isle of Man Data Protection Act 2018 and the applied GDPR.

Who we are

KDO Digital is a one-person web design business on the Isle of Man, run by Kyle Ormesher. It builds websites and related services for businesses. For any data-related questions, contact us at kyle@kdodigital.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and run our service:

  • Information you give us — such as your name, email address, your website address and the contents of any message you send through the free website check form or by email.
  • Technical information — basic, non-identifying information your browser shares automatically (such as device type and general location) may be processed by our hosting provider to keep the site secure and reliable.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To provide and improve the services you ask us for.
  • To keep records of our correspondence and agreements.

We will never sell your data, and we will not send you marketing you did not ask for.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business, and on the basis of consent where you have actively contacted us. Where we enter into a project, we also process data to perform our contract with you.

Sharing your data

We use trusted third-party providers to operate (for example, website hosting and email). Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. These providers only process your data on our instructions and under their own data protection obligations. We do not share your data with anyone else unless required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as necessary to provide our service and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to request a copy of it. To exercise any of these rights, email kyle@kdodigital.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies or tracking. Its fonts and files are served from this site itself. If we add analytics in future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will always show the current version.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email kyle@kdodigital.co.uk.
