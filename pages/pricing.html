Questions

Common questions

Are these prices fixed or estimated?

The tiers above are starting points. Every project is scoped and quoted individually before any work begins. You always know the exact price before we start.

What does the monthly retainer cover?

Hosting, uptime monitoring, security updates, content changes, bug fixes, performance optimisation, and monthly reporting. Small changes are included — large new features are quoted separately.

Do I need to pay for hosting separately?

No. Hosting on Vercel and the database on Supabase are managed as part of your retainer. You don't deal with servers, renewals, or technical infrastructure.

Which payment methods do you accept for client payments?

PayPal or SumUp only for any payment systems built into your project. We never handle card data directly, and we never integrate Stripe for Isle of Man clients.

How long does a project take?

A standard website takes 2–4 weeks from sign-off to launch. More complex projects with booking systems, apps, or integrations typically take 4–8 weeks.

What happens if I want to cancel the retainer?

30 days' notice. You own everything we build — your code, your data, your domain. We'll hand over everything cleanly and help you transition if needed.
